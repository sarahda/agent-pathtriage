\tool, the analysis, the harness that produced every artifact, and the lab
that builds and tears down the live demonstration are available at
\url{https://github.com/sarahda/agent-pathtriage}.

Every number in this paper is defined once in \texttt{numbers.tex}, and each
definition names the artifact it was read from. A single command,
\texttt{python3 evaluation/\allowbreak reproduce.py}, re-derives the published
figures from the committed artifacts and fails if any of them disagrees with
the paper, so a reader can check that the two agree without rerunning the
collection. Adding \texttt{-{}-full} re-walks the corpus itself. The live demonstration creates and deletes its own AWS
resources and reports the names of credential sources rather than their
values.
