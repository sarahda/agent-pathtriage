QUERY HELP:

First form:
   can <Principal> do <Action> [with <Resource [when <ConditionA> [and <ConditionB>...]]]
Second form:
   who can do <Action> [with <Resource> [when <ConditionA> [and <ConditionB>...]]]
Third form:
   preset <preset args>

Available presets:
    * connected (principal|"*") (principal|"*")
    * privesc (principal|"*")
    * clusters (tag key)
    * endgame (service|"*")
    * serviceaccess
    * wrongadmin
